Understanding Organ Donation

Organ donation is the process of giving a part of your body (organ) to someone who needs a transplant due to end-stage organ disease. Vital organs such as the heart, lungs, kidneys, liver, pancreas, and intestines can be donated, along with tissues like corneas, heart valves, skin, and bone. Each donation has the potential to save or significantly improve the life of a recipient.

Our platform supports both living and deceased donations, with comprehensive verification systems to ensure the authenticity and suitability of all donations. Through our blockchain infrastructure, we maintain complete transparency while protecting the privacy of all participants.

Legal Framework and Compliance

Organ donation in India is governed by the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994, which establishes strict guidelines for both living and deceased donations. Our platform is designed to comply with all legal requirements while providing additional security through blockchain verification.

The National Organ and Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO) serves as the apex body for organ procurement and distribution. Our platform works in conjunction with existing regulatory frameworks, providing enhanced tracking and verification capabilities that complement the current system.

Types of Donors Supported

Living Donors

Living donors can donate specific organs while maintaining their health and quality of life:

  • One kidney (the body can function normally with one kidney)
  • Part of the liver (the liver can regenerate)
  • Portion of the pancreas
  • Part of the lung

Living donors must be over 18 years of age and undergo comprehensive medical evaluation to ensure their safety and the suitability of their donation.

Deceased Donors

Deceased donation occurs after brain death or cardiac death, allowing for the donation of multiple organs and tissues. Our platform maintains detailed records of donor consent and medical suitability, ensuring that all donations are properly documented and verified.

Important Facts About Organ Donation

  • Anyone can be an organ donor regardless of age, race, or medical history (final determination is made at the time of death)
  • Organ donation does not interfere with funeral arrangements - donors can have open-casket funerals
  • There is no cost to the donor's family for organ donation
  • One donor can save up to 8 lives through organ donation and improve many more through tissue donation
  • Religious beliefs are respected, and most major religions support organ donation
  • Medical professionals always prioritize saving the donor's life - organ donation is only considered after all life-saving efforts have failed

The Donation Process on Our Platform

Phase 1: Registration and Data Storage

Donors, patients, and pledges can register on our platform with their medical information. All data is securely stored on the blockchain using smart contracts, ensuring immutability and transparency. Each registration includes personal details, medical information, and organ preferences.

Phase 2: Data Validation and Verification

Our smart contracts automatically validate the authenticity of submitted data and prevent duplicate registrations. Authorized verifiers can review and approve donor and patient registrations, ensuring the quality and accuracy of the database.

Phase 3: AI-Powered Matching and Compatibility Analysis

Our platform uses advanced machine learning algorithms to automatically match donors with patients. The AI system analyzes blood type compatibility, age factors, organ requirements, and medical criteria to generate match scores and recommendations. The system combines AI predictions with traditional medical compatibility rules to provide the most accurate matching results.

Phase 4: Pledge to Donor Conversion

Our platform supports the conversion of pledges to actual donors. When a pledge is ready to become a donor, their information can be transferred from the pledge registry to the donor registry, maintaining the complete audit trail on the blockchain.

Phase 5: Verification and Compliance

All donor and patient registrations can be verified by authorized medical professionals. The verification status is recorded on the blockchain, ensuring compliance with medical and legal requirements while maintaining transparency.
